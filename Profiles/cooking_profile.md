# Cooking Profile

- **Learning Style:** Prefers structured, low-stress, step-by-step guidance.
- **Needs:** Sanity checks and precise timing/ratios.
- **Current Focus:** Building fundamental kitchen skills (knife work, prep, zero-burn grains).

---

## Preferences

- **Goal:** Edible basic meals, not haute cuisine. Focus on simple, reproducible results.
- **Approach:** Introduce exactly **one** new variable at a time (e.g., one new vegetable, one sauce, or assembly of basic elements).
- **Pace:** Low-stress, slow, and incremental. Step-by-step progression to manage cooking anxiety and avoid freeze-ups. Avoid rushing or overwhelming combinations.
