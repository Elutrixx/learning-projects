# User & Hardware Profile

## Student Profile
- Prefers structured, low-stress, step-by-step guidance.
- Needs visual sanity checks (photos) and precise timing/ratios.
- Focus: Building fundamental kitchen skills (knife work, prep, zero-burn grains).

## Hardware Setup
- **Stove:** Electric glass cooktop (Settings 1 to 6, where 1 is lowest simmer and 6 is max high).
- **Primary Pot:** Stainless steel pot with snug-fitting metal lid (approx. 20–22 cm diameter).
- **Tools Available:** Digital scale, standard mugs/cups, kitchen knives, cutting board.
