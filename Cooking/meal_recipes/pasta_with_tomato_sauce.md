# Recipe: Simple Pasta with Tomato Sauce

A reliable, classic comforting meal. 

## Equipment Needed
- 1 medium pot
- Colander/Strainer
- Stirring spoon or spatula

## Ingredients & Ratios
- **Pasta:** 100g-150g Italian dried pasta (e.g., Penne or Spaghetti)
- **Sauce:** 1 jar (~400g) store-bought Tomato/Marinara Sauce
- **Fat/Flavor (Optional):** 1 tsp olive oil or butter, grated cheese

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Cook the Pasta (~11 mins)
1. Boil water on **Dial 6** in your medium pot. Add 1 tsp salt.
2. Cook pasta according to package instructions (usually 9–11 minutes) until soft with a slight bite (al dente).
3. Drain the pasta in a colander in the sink. **Do not rinse.**

### Step 2: Heat the Sauce & Combine (~4 mins)
1. Turn heat down to **Dial 2** (low).
2. Pour the jar of tomato sauce into the empty, warm pot.
3. Add the optional tsp of olive oil or butter.
4. Heat the sauce for 2 minutes until it begins to steam and bubble gently.
5. Add the drained pasta back into the pot with the sauce. Stir gently for 1 minute to coat the pasta completely.

## Technical Notes
- **Why salt the pasta water?** It is the only chance to season the inside of the noodle itself, preventing a bland tasting meal.
- **Why not rinse the pasta?** Rinsing washes away the surface starches. These starches act like glue, helping the tomato sauce stick to the noodles instead of sliding off.
