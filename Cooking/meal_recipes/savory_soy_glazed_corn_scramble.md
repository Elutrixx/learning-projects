# Recipe: Savory Soy-Glazed Corn Scramble

A simple, highly satisfying, and salty-savory meal that transforms canned corn and eggs into a comforting breakfast or quick lunch.

## Equipment Needed
- 1 medium skillet (non-stick)
- 1 small bowl (for whisking eggs)
- Fork (for whisking)
- Spatula (silicone or wooden preferred)

## Ingredients & Ratios
- **Eggs:** 3-4 large eggs (perfect for utilizing your leftovers!)
- **Canned Corn:** 1/2 can of corn (about 100g - 150g), thoroughly drained
- **Cooking Oil:** 2 tsp of any neutral cooking oil (vegetable, canola, sunflower, etc.)
- **Savory Glaze:** 1 tbsp soy sauce
- **Seasoning:** A pinch of black pepper (and optionally a tiny pinch of garlic powder)
- **Garnish (Optional):** Sliced spring onion (using *Spring Onion* raw garnish method)

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Prep & Whisk (~5 mins)
1. **Drain Corn:** Open your can of corn. Use the lid to press and drain out all of the liquid. Measure out about half of the can and set aside. (Keep the rest in a sealed container in the fridge).
2. **Whisk Eggs:** Crack 3 or 4 eggs into your small bowl. Add a tiny pinch of black pepper. Whisk vigorously with a fork for 30 seconds until the whites and yolks are fully blended and a little frothy. Do *not* add salt yet (the soy sauce added later will provide the salt).

### Step 2: Sear the Corn (~5 mins)
1. **Heat Pan:** Add 1 tsp of cooking oil to your non-stick skillet. Set your stove to **Dial 4** (medium-high). Let the pan and oil heat up for 1–2 minutes.
2. **Sauté Corn:** Carefully add the drained corn to the pan. Sauté the corn for 3–4 minutes, stirring occasionally.
    - *Sanity check:* Look for some kernels to get slightly blistered and golden-brown.
3. **Glaze Corn:** Pour 1 tbsp of soy sauce directly over the hot corn. It will hiss and steam immediately! Stir rapidly for 30–45 seconds so the soy sauce coats the corn and reduces into a savory, sticky glaze.
4. **Transfer:** Tip the glazed corn out of the pan onto a clean plate. 

### Step 3: Scramble the Eggs (~2-3 mins)
1. **Cool & Lower Heat:** Keep the skillet on the stove, but turn the heat down to **Dial 2** (low). Let the pan cool down for about 30 seconds so it is not too hot.
2. **Add Oil & Eggs:** Add the second tsp of cooking oil to the pan. Pour in your whisked eggs.
3. **Scramble Gently:** Let the eggs sit undisturbed for 10–15 seconds until they just start to set around the edges. Using your spatula, gently push the set edges toward the center. Repeat this slowly.
4. **Fold in Corn:** When the eggs are about 70% cooked (still wet and glossy, but mostly holding together), dump the glazed corn back into the pan.
5. **Finish Cook:** Gently fold the corn and eggs together for 15–30 seconds. Turn the stove **completely OFF**. The residual heat of the pan will finish cooking the eggs so they stay tender and do not dry out.

### Step 4: Plate & Serve
1. Slide the scramble onto a plate.
2. Garnish with fresh spring onion greens if you have them. Enjoy hot!

## Technical Notes
- **Why no salt in the whisked eggs?** Soy sauce is highly savory and salty. Glazing the corn first allows the saltiness to concentrate, which seasons the entire dish when combined.
- **Why remove the corn before scrambling?** If you pour eggs directly into a hot pan full of corn, the pan is too hot and the eggs will overcook instantly, turning rubbery. Cooking them separately on low heat guarantees perfectly tender scrambled eggs.
- **Why turn the stove off early?** Eggs continue cooking even after being plated. Turning off the heat while they still look slightly wet ensures they are silky, moist, and perfect by the time you eat.
