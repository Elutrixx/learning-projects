# Recipe: Quick Teriyaki Chicken Wraps

A clean, crisp wrap combining warm glazed chicken with fresh vegetables.

## Equipment Needed
- 1 medium skillet
- Cutting board & sharp chef's knife
- Spatula or tongs

## Ingredients & Ratios
- **Tortilla:** 1-2 large flour tortillas
- **Protein:** 1 Chicken Breast, prepped into strips or cubes (approx 150g)
- **Veggie Crunch:** 1 cup prepped Iceberg Salad
- **Garnish:** 1 Spring Onion, sliced thin
- **Sauce:** 2-3 tbsp Teriyaki Sauce (store-bought)
- **Fat:** 1 tbsp cooking oil

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Prep & Sauté Chicken (~10 mins)
1. Prep your chicken, iceberg salad, and spring onions using the standard guides.
2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in the skillet on **Dial 4** (medium-high) for 2 minutes.
3. Cook chicken for 6–7 minutes until fully cooked through and golden.

### Step 2: Glaze the Chicken (~2 mins)
1. Drop stove heat to **Dial 2** (low).
2. Pour 2-3 tbsp of Teriyaki sauce over the chicken.
3. Stir for 1 minute until sauce is warm, sticky, and coats every piece. Remove skillet from heat.

### Step 3: Assemble the Wrap (~2 mins)
1. Lay tortilla flat.
2. Place a handful of Iceberg salad in the center line.
3. Spoon the warm Teriyaki chicken over the salad.
4. Sprinkle sliced spring onions on top.
5. Fold the sides in and roll tightly. Cut in half if desired.

## Technical Notes
- **Why wrap layering matters:** Placing the cold iceberg salad down first protects the tortilla from getting soggy from the warm, saucy chicken.
