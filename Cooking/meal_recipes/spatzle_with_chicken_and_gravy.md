# Recipe: Spätzle with Chicken & Gravy

An easy, comforting German-inspired dish using pre-packaged Spätzle.

## Equipment Needed
- 1 medium pot (to boil/warm Spätzle)
- 1 medium skillet (for chicken & gravy)
- Strainer
- Spatula or spoon

## Ingredients & Ratios
- **Carb:** 1 package (~250g) store-bought fresh Spätzle
- **Protein:** 1 Chicken Breast (~150-200g), diced/strips
- **Gravy:** 1 jar or packet of prepared chicken/poultry gravy (German: *Geflügelsoße*)
- **Fat:** 1 tbsp butter (or cooking oil)
- **Seasoning:** Salt and black pepper

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Cook the Chicken (~7 mins)
1. Heat 1 tbsp butter/oil in a skillet on **Dial 4** (medium-high) for 2 minutes.
2. Sauté the chicken pieces for 6-7 minutes until fully cooked through and golden. Set chicken aside on a clean plate.

### Step 2: Prepare the Spätzle (~5 mins)
*Fresh Spätzle from the store are already pre-cooked and only need warming up!*
* **The Quick Method:** 
  1. Bring a medium pot of salted water to a rolling boil on **Dial 6**.
  2. Drop the Spätzle in. 
  3. Let them cook for **2–3 minutes** (they are done when they float to the surface).
  4. Strain the water.

### Step 3: Combine with Gravy (~3 mins)
1. Lower the skillet heat to **Dial 2** (low).
2. Pour your prepared chicken gravy into the warm skillet. 
3. Return the cooked chicken to the skillet. Stir gently until hot and bubbling (about 2 minutes).
4. Serve the hot chicken and gravy directly over the warm Spätzle.

## Technical Notes
- **What is Spätzle?** A soft egg noodle style pasta traditional to Southern Germany, Austria, and Switzerland. Fresh, store-bought Spätzle are delicate and only need brief heating; overcooking will make them mushy.
- **Why use butter instead of oil?** Butter pairs naturally with the rich egg flavor of Spätzle and makes the chicken taste richer and more traditional.
