# Recipe: The Idiot-Proof White Rice Method

## Equipment Needed
- Pot with tight-fitting lid
- Standard mug/cup (or digital scale)
- Fork for fluffing

## Ingredients & Ratios
- **White Rice:** 1 mug (e.g., Jasmine or Basmati, ~250g)
- **Water:** 1.5 mugs (cold tap water, ~375ml)
- **Extras:** 1 pinch salt + 1 tsp butter or oil

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Rinse:** Swish rice in pot with cold water for 5 seconds; carefully pour off cloudy water.
2. **Boil (Lid OFF):** Add 1.5x fresh water, salt, and butter. High heat (Dial 6) until a **rolling boil** is reached.
3. **Simmer (Lid ON) — 12 MINS:** Drop heat to lowest setting (Dial 1). Cover tightly. Set timer for **12 minutes**.
    - *Rule:* Do NOT lift the lid, do NOT stir, do NOT peek.
4. **Steam Rest — 10 MINS:** Turn stove completely off. Move pot to a cold burner. Keep lid CLOSED. Set timer for **10 minutes**.
5. **Finish:** Uncover, fluff gently with a fork, serve.

## Technical Notes
- **Why no stirring?** Prevents releasing starch paste that sinks, traps heat, and burns the bottom.
- **Why rest off heat?** Gentle trapped steam cooks the top layer while allowing the pot base to cool so nothing scorches.
