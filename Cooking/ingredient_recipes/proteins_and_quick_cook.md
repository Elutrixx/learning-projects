# Proteins Reference

### Chicken (Diced or Strips)
* **Prep:** 1/2-inch strips or 1-inch cubes.
* **Cook (Pan-Fry):** Dial 4 with 1 tbsp oil. Cook undisturbed for 3 mins, stir/cook 3-4 mins. (Sanity check: white center).

### Scrambled Eggs
* **Prep:** Whisked with salt + splash of milk/water.
* **Cook:** Dial 2 with 1 tsp butter. Push edges to center for 1-2 mins until soft but set.

### Hot Dogs
* **Cook (Boil):** Boil water on Dial 6, slide in hot dogs, turn stove OFF, cover for 5-7 mins.
* **Cook (Pan-Fry):** Score diagonally, Dial 3 with 1 tsp oil for 5-6 mins.
