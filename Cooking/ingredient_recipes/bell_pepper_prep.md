# Ingredient: Pan-Fried Bell Pepper Strips

## Equipment Needed
- Cutting board & sharp chef's knife
- Medium skillet or pan
- Spatula or tongs

## Ingredients & Ratios
- **Bell Pepper:** 1 medium (any color)
- **Fat:** 1 tsp cooking oil (or butter)
- **Seasoning:** A pinch of salt

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Prep & Knife Work (~5 mins)
1. **Wash** the bell pepper and pat it dry.
2. **Cut** the top and bottom off the pepper. Stand it upright on your cutting board.
3. **Slice** down one side of the wall. Roll the pepper out flat on the board and slide your knife horizontally to slice out the white ribs and seeds in one piece. Discard ribs and seeds.
4. **Slice** the flat pepper panel into uniform 1/2-inch strips using the claw grip to protect your fingers.

### Step 2: Pan-Fry (~5 mins)
1. **Heat Pan:** Add 1 tsp oil to your skillet. Turn heat to **Dial 4** (medium-high). Let the pan get hot for 1-2 minutes.
2. **Cook:** Add the pepper strips and a pinch of salt to the pan. Cook them, moving them around with your spatula every 30 seconds so they don't burn.
3. **Check:** After ~5 minutes, they should get slightly soft but still have a bit of crispness. Remove from heat.

## Technical Notes
- **Why remove top, bottom, and roll?** This flat-roll method is the safest, most efficient way to get clean, seed-free strips with minimal waste.
- **What is "Pan-frying"?** Cooking food in a pan with a small amount of fat/oil over medium-to-high heat, stirring occasionally to distribute heat evenly.
