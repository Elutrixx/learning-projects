# Produce & Pantry Reference

### Iceberg Salad
* **Prep:** Chopped / shredded.
* **Cook:** None (Raw).

### Avocado
* **How to Buy:** Squeeze gently in your palm. Hard like a rock = unripe. Slightly soft/yielding (like pressing the fleshy base of your thumb) = perfectly ripe. Mushy/sinking in = overripe.
* **Prep:**
  1. **Cut:** Run a chef's knife lengthwise around the center until you hit the pit. Twist the two halves in opposite directions to separate.
  2. **De-seed:** Gently slide a spoon underneath the pit and pop it out. *(Safety check: Do NOT strike the pit with a knife edge to twist it out. This is a common cause of kitchen injuries!).*
  3. **Slice:** Scoop the entire green flesh out with a spoon, lay it flat-side down on the cutting board, and slice into 1/4-inch strips.
* **Cook:** None (Raw).

### Spring Onion
* **Prep:** Sliced thin (whites & greens).
* **Cook (Raw):** Garnish.

### Bell Pepper
* **Prep:** 1/2-inch strips.
* **Cook (Pan-Fry):** Dial 4 with 1 tsp oil for 5 mins (soft but crisp).

### Pickles
* **Prep:** Rounds or diced.
* **Cook:** None.

### Canned Corn
* **Prep:** Drain thoroughly (using the can lid or a strainer).
* **Cook (Pan-Fry):** Dial 4 with 1 tsp oil for 4–5 mins until some kernels turn golden brown.

### Bread
* **Cook (Pan-Toast):** Dial 3 with 1 tsp butter for 2 mins, flip for 1–2 mins.

### Italian Pasta (Dried)
* **Cook (Boil):** Dial 6 (boiling water) + 1 tsp salt. Boil uncovered for 8–11 mins. (Sanity check: soft with tiny chew).
